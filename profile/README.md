**Gyyym is your AI gym buddy.** Walk in knowing exactly what to do.

- **Plan and log your training:** today's workout, sets, rest timer, records and history.
- **Body and progress:** weight, measurements and InBody scans, next to your training.
- **Honest, kind summaries:** after every workout, a short take where every number comes from your own data.
- **Connect your AI:** use the assistant you already have (Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini) with your training data, with your consent.

On iOS, Android and the web.

**Status:** early development. We are building the foundations.
